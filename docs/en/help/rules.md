This page applies to comments on this site, to the chat group, and to any conversation held in the name of MCSOG. The goal is simple: people who ask questions get answers, and people who answer want to keep answering.

## Ground rules

- Be friendly: criticise the work, not the person; no personal attacks, no regional discrimination, no mocking beginners
- Respect copyright: do not post pirated resources, cracking tools, cheats or ways to bypass official account checks
- Do not spam: do not repeat the same message, do not mention people back to back, do not post meaningless characters
- Do not advertise: no server promotion, paid hosting or paid maintenance without permission
- Stay lawful: do not discuss or spread anything that breaks the laws of your jurisdiction

## How to ask

"Help, my server will not start" cannot be answered. Please provide as much of the following as you can:

- the **server core and version** you use (for example Paper 1.21.4)
- the **way you start it** (panel, systemd, command line)
- the **complete error log** (paste the text, not just a screenshot, and not just the last line)
- what you have **already tried**, and what happened

## Editing and submissions

- Fixing typos, adding command output and updating stale version numbers are all welcome as direct submissions
- New guides should state the target reader and the prerequisites first, rather than only saying "click here, then click there"
- When third-party software is involved, link to the official download page instead of a file-hosting mirror
- For the full editing conventions see "Editing Guide" and "Contribution"

## Handling violations

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| First minor violation (spamming, going off topic) | A warning and removal of the content |
| Repeated violations or advertising | Muted, with past content cleaned up |
| Posting pirated, cheating or illegal content | Removed outright, with no appeal |
| Impersonating another person or the site staff | Removed outright |

## Changes and disclaimer

These rules are adjusted as circumstances require, and the latest version on this page always applies.

The **only official chat group** for this site is the QQ group [1083430859](https://qm.qq.com/q/nRsqCiwHMm), and these rules apply there as well. There is no WeChat group, Discord or Telegram group. Anyone who invites you to another group in this site's name, asks for your account or offers paid hosting is unconnected to us; do not trust them.

If you disagree with a moderation decision, you can explain your case in the group.
