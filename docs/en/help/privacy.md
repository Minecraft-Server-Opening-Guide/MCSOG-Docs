This page explains what data this site collects, why, how long it is kept, and how you can ask for deletion.

## What we collect

:::step Account information
When you sign in with a third-party account we store only the user identifier, display name and avatar URL returned by the provider, so your browser can show a signed-in state. We **never store your password** and cannot access your other data at the provider.
:::

:::step Required cookies
`mcsog_session` keeps you signed in, `mcsog_lang` remembers the interface language and `mcsog_theme` (in browser storage) remembers light or dark mode. They are required for the site to work and are not used for advertising.
:::

:::step Access logs
The server records time, IP, request path and User-Agent for diagnostics and abuse prevention, retained for no longer than 30 days.
:::

## What we never do

- Sell or trade any personal data;
- Load third-party advertising or behavioural tracking scripts;
- Read your browsing history on other sites.

## Third parties

- **OAuth provider**: handles authentication under its own privacy policy;
- **Object storage / CDN**: serves downloads only and never receives your account data;
- **AI chat widget**: if enabled, it is provided by a third party whose policy applies.

:::note
This site is not intended for children under 13. If we learn of such an account we delete its data.
:::

## Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of data linked to your account by writing to the address on the contact page. We respond within 15 working days.
