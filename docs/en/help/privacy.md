This page explains what data this site collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how you can exercise your rights. The itemised list of cookies and browser storage entries is on the [Cookie Usage](/en/help/cookies) page.

## Who we are

MCSOG (mcsog.ndpreforged.com) is a community-maintained, unofficial Minecraft server tutorial and resource index. It does not provide official Minecraft support and cannot handle account bans or ownership verification. This policy covers both the Chinese site and the English site under `/en`.

## What we collect

:::step Sign-in information
When you sign in with a third-party account, the authentication provider returns a user identifier, display name, avatar URL and access token. We keep them in the server session so later requests stay signed in. The session is destroyed when you sign out or when it expires, we keep **no separate account database**, we **never store your password**, and we cannot reach your other data at the provider.
:::

:::step Content you send us
When you email or message us a correction, submission, report or link exchange, we see the text, attachments and contact details you provide and use them only to handle that request.
:::

:::step Access logs
The server records request time, IP, request path and User-Agent for diagnostics, rate limiting and abuse prevention.
:::

:::step Preferences on your device
Language, theme, sidebar, read announcements and agreement records stay in your own browser and are never uploaded to the server.
:::

## How we use it

- Keep you signed in and render the language and theme you picked;
- Diagnose faults and limit abusive or malicious requests;
- Measure traffic and page performance to decide which tutorials need work (only after you accept third-party cookies);
- Answer your corrections, submissions, reports and link requests.

## Cookies and similar technologies

The site uses three categories of cookies: **essential** (session, consent state, scroll position and similar, which cannot be turned off), **optional** (preferences such as language, theme, sidebar, read announcements and agreement records) and **third-party** (traffic analytics and performance monitoring, loaded only after you accept them). What each key does, where it lives and how to clear it is listed on [Cookie Usage](/en/help/cookies). You can also open "Cookie settings" in the footer at any time to review, change or withdraw consent; changes take effect the next time a page loads.

## Sharing and third parties

- **BuilddreamsAuth**: handles authentication under its own privacy policy;
- **Object storage / CDN**: serves downloads only and never receives your account data;
- **Third-party analytics**: provides traffic statistics and page performance analysis, and sets cookies and local storage to tell visitors apart; **it only loads after you accept third-party cookies**, and you can withdraw that consent at any time from cookie preferences;
- **AI chat widget**: if enabled, it is provided by a separately deployed service whose own notice applies.

We run **no third-party advertising scripts** and never sell or trade your personal data. Apart from what the law requires or an effective legal process demands, we do not disclose your data to unrelated third parties.

## Data retention

| Data | Retained for |
|---|---|
| Sign-in session | Until it expires or you sign out |
| Access logs | No longer than 30 days |
| Correction, submission and report threads | Cleared within 90 days after the request is closed |
| Preferences and consent record in your browser | Stay on your device until you clear them |

## Your rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of data linked to your account, and you may withdraw consent for optional and third-party cookies at any time. Write to the address on the contact page or to [mcsog@ndpreforged.com](mailto:mcsog@ndpreforged.com); we respond within 15 working days.

## Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update the date at the top of the page; material changes to what we collect or who we share it with are also announced in the news section. Continuing to use the site means you accept the updated version.

## Contact

Email [mcsog@ndpreforged.com](mailto:mcsog@ndpreforged.com), or see the [contact page](/en/contact) for other channels. For copyright complaints, include proof of ownership and the exact link.
