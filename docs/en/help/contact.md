If you have a problem, have spotted a mistake, or want to submit a resource or a guide, reach us through the channels below.

## How to reach us

:::step Corrections
Note the page path, the exact paragraph and your suggested fix, then send them to the mailbox below; start the subject with "Correction".
:::

:::step Resources and friend links
Provide the resource name, official homepage, version and licence details; for a friend link, include the site name, the URL and a one-line description.
:::

:::step Infringement and reporting
Provide proof of rights and the exact links; we will act once we have verified them.

:::note
This site is an unofficial site maintained by the player community. It does not offer official Minecraft support and cannot handle official matters such as account bans or ownership verification.
:::

## Response times

| Type | Expected response |
|---|---|
| Infringement reports | Within 3 working days |
| Corrections | Within 7 working days |
| Friend links and submissions | Within 7 working days |

## Official community group

The **only official community group** for this site is the QQ group [1083430859](https://qm.qq.com/q/nRsqCiwHMm); click the group number to join.

There is no WeChat group, Discord, Telegram or any other group. Anyone who invites you into another group in this site's name, asks for your account or charges a fee to host your server has nothing to do with this site.

Before you join, have a look at the "Community Rules"; when you ask a question, include your server core and version, how you start the server and the complete log, which makes a useful answer much more likely.

:::warn
The contact mailbox will be announced once the site is officially running; until then, for anything formal (infringement, partnerships, friend links), state what you need in the group first.
:::
