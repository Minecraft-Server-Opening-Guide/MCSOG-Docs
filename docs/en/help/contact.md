Reach us for problems, corrections, or to submit resources and guides.

## How to reach us

:::step Corrections
Send the page path, the exact paragraph and your suggested fix, with a subject starting with "Correction".
:::

:::step Resources and links
Include the resource name, official homepage, version and licence. For friend links add the site name, URL and a one-line description.
:::

:::step Infringement reports
Include proof of rights and the exact URL; we act after verification.

:::note
This site is a community-run unofficial site. We cannot handle official Minecraft support such as account bans or ownership verification.
:::

## Response times

| Type | Expected response |
|---|---|
| Infringement reports | Within 3 working days |
| Corrections | Within 7 working days |
| Links and submissions | Within 7 working days |

:::warn
The contact mailbox will be published once the site is officially launched. Until then please use the repository issue tracker.
:::
